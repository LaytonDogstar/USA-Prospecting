How it works

The portal maps the US lending lead-gen market: lenders that buy leads, the traffic sources that sell them, who owns whom, and where we already work. Machines do the gathering (spreadsheet imports, research with Claude, Ahrefs traffic, Hunter contacts). People decide what to believe.

  • Every fact has a source. Click the small i next to any fact to see where it came from.
  • Nothing is merged, linked or promoted without a decision, made by a person or, for clear-cut cases only, by the assistant. Every decision is logged with its reason.
  • "Our partner" is only ever set by a person (from our relationships list), never guessed from a name or website.

Your 10-minute routine

  1. Notifications (the bell, also Needs you on Home): anything that failed, hit a budget or needs your decision. Notices close themselves once dealt with.
  2. Report: what ran, what it cost, what the assistant decided (with reasons) and what is waiting.
  3. Review: work through what's left. Most cards carry an Assistant recommends line; press Confirm if it is right, or choose another button and add a note.
  4. Prospects: open one or two "not yet worked" organisations from Home, read the page and its sources, and check People → Ready to contact.

Weekly: look at Leverage and the Map.

The Claude team

  • Researchers (Sonnet for new companies, Opus for follow-up questions) look up each company on the web: lender type, owner, brands, loan range, states, partner lists, compliance notes. Every fact must quote a page they actually opened, or it is dropped. Facts arrive as proposed on a research card.
  • The manager (the daily assistant, Opus) reads every open card. It applies only the clear-cut safe cases, recommends on the rest, and can send a card back to the researchers with up to three questions. The answers return on a new card linked to the original, and the manager looks again.
  • The daily Claude review signs in as the user claude, works through the inbox and posts a short report to Notifications. It never changes users, budgets, weights or imports.

You can send any card or organisation back yourself: Ask the researchers on a review card or organisation page. Choose a focus: owners and partners, the company profile, or decision-makers (names and titles).

Deciding review cards

Type a note before you click: it goes in the audit log. A green safe chip is a clear case; check first means look before applying; no suggestion means it is your call.

Possible matches (do these first)

  • Same: keep left / right: one company with two records. The relationship carries over.
  • Left owns right / Right owns left: a holding company and its trading brand. Links them instead of merging.
  • Related: keep held back: a sister brand, servicer or same-tribe lender of a partner. Not the same company, but nobody should cold-call it.
  • Not related: a lookalike. It goes back on the prospect lists.

Duplicates

The same choices. Watch "same name, different websites": two companies, or a rebrand.

Research findings

  • Accept these facts: they are used and the organisation is re-scored. Approved decision-makers become people.
  • Accept and screen out: keep the facts, take it off the lender and supply lists (apps, banks, non-lenders).
  • Reject: discard the facts. With nothing found, the company is researched again later.
  • Follow-up cards show the questions and answers at the top, each with its source.

If a role looks wrong after accepting, use Remove next to it on the organisation page. If a company was screened out by mistake, use Undo screen-out on its card.

Low confidence and new entrants

Approve a spreadsheet value if it looks right, reject it if not. New entrants are sites seen in search results for the first time: decide whether each is a lender, a traffic source or irrelevant.

The partner safeguard

An organisation is held back, flagged may already be a partner and never looked up for contacts, when it has an open possible match, was marked Related: keep held back, owns one of our partners, or is owned by one. Don't contact it until that is resolved.

Contacts

For prospects scoring 60+ that pass the partner safeguard, aren't blocked or screened out and have a website, Hunter looks up the people it knows at that website. We keep up to 3, by title:

  1. Partnerships / affiliates / business development
  2. Lead acquisition, performance or digital marketing
  3. CMO, marketing, growth
  4. CEO, founder, president, owner, general manager
  5. COO, chief revenue officer, head of sales

Never kept: shared inboxes (info@, sales@), invalid addresses, technology, finance, legal, HR, risk and customer-service roles, and roles outside the US. When Hunter finds nobody suitable, the researchers look for named decision-makers on company pages, press releases and filings (never LinkedIn); once you accept their card, the next contacts run finds the emails.

  • Email chips: verified, catch-all (the mail server accepts any address, so it can't be confirmed), unverified.
  • Find contacts on an organisation page looks it up now. Do not contact works for a person or a whole organisation; they are kept on record so they are never looked up again.
  • To delete a person (for example, a deletion request), use Delete with the reason. Their email goes with them; the audit log keeps only the name and reason.
  • Names found by hand in Sales Navigator can be added under Add a person.

Notifications

The bell shows unread notices: failed or skipped jobs, budget warnings, a review summary and the daily Claude report. A notice closes for everyone when its cards are decided, a newer report replaces it, or the job has since run cleanly. Admins can close one by hand with Resolved.

Budgets and keys

Each provider (Anthropic, Ahrefs, Hunter) has a monthly cap under Research & budgets. Every job checks it before each paid call and stops cleanly (budget_stopped) instead of going over. API keys live in Railway → USA-Prospecting → Variables, never in the portal or in chat; the page only shows whether each is set. Check Ahrefs plan and Check Hunter account are free.

Pages

  • Home: what needs you, the best buyers and traffic sources we don't work with yet, recent changes.
  • Lenders / Supply / People: sortable tables with filters and CSV export.
  • Organisation page: every fact with its source, scores and their parts, roles, relationships, people.
  • Map: traffic sources → connectors → pingtrees → lenders. Owner groups fold; click to expand. Blue rings are possible partners.
  • States: which states lenders serve. Leverage: owner groups and sources feeding several of our buyers.

Chips and words

  • live / paused / contracting / in talks / former: our relationship, set only by a person.
  • Compliance blocked: hidden from ranked lists. review: shown with a warning.
  • Score 25 ("unknown"): no data yet for that part of the score, so thin records don't outrank researched ones.
  • proposed / accepted: a fact waiting for a decision, or one in use.

If something goes wrong

  • A run failed: the notice links to it; the error is in Recent runs on Research & budgets.
  • budget_stopped: the monthly cap was reached. Raise it, or wait for next month.
  • A schedule says skipped: its API key isn't set in Railway, or it was already running.
  • A wrong merge or screen-out: note the card number (on the card and in the audit log) and ask Claude; screen-outs can be undone from the card.
  • Numbers look off after uploading files in the wrong order: Admin → Imports → Reset and re-import. It clears decisions, so do it before working through Review.
  • The site is down: check the service in Railway. Backups run daily and weekly.
